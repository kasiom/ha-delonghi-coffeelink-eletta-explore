# Branding

The integration uses an original, unofficial icon created for this independent
Home Assistant project. It does not reproduce the De'Longhi or Coffee Link logo.

The selected design combines three functional cues:

- a front-facing automatic coffee machine,
- a prepared espresso,
- three wireless-connection arcs.

The navy body remains legible in both light and dark Home Assistant themes. The
warm coffee and coral connection accents stay distinguishable at small sizes.
The production PNG files have a real transparent alpha channel and no baked-in
background.

Files:

- `custom_components/ha_delonghi_coffeelink_eletta_explore/brand/icon.png`
  (256 × 256 px),
- `custom_components/ha_delonghi_coffeelink_eletta_explore/brand/icon@2x.png`
  (512 × 512 px).

The source was generated in ImageGen and then technically prepared for Home
Assistant by removing the chroma background, trimming, centering, resizing and
checking the alpha channel. QA includes previews on light and dark backgrounds.

Generation brief:

> Minimal flat vector-style integration icon, front-facing automatic coffee
> machine in deep navy, compact espresso cup with warm brown coffee, three coral
> wireless arcs above the machine, centered composition, no text, no brand logo,
> simple bold shapes, legible at 48 px, transparent background.
